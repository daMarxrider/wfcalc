Assumptions & warnings

  • All mods at max rank, 60 capacity (Orokin Catalyst).
  • Necrophagic Vigor at the 360% cap (conditions: all).
  • The Exilus and stance slots are not searched.
  • Parts you did not tick are never used. Combinations are ranked with the mods the first part wants.
  • Heavy Caliber has an unmodelled accuracy penalty; the search prefers an equal alternative when one fits.
  • GPU search failed, ran on the CPU instead (Vulkan device lost).
  • Unknown effects on 1 mod: the result is a lower bound.
