Everything else comes from the Build tab: target, loadout, arcanes, Incarnon perks, headshots and the Conditions & stacks panel (tick “aiming” for Primary Compression). Looking for a GPU…

The GPU scores every combination of up to 64 candidate mods with a reduced damage model and scores the best 20,000 with the full engine. Much faster on big searches; a build the reduced model badly underrates can be missed. Works on AMD, Nvidia, Intel and Apple GPUs. Saved in this browser only.
