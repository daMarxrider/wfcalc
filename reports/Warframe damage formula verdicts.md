# wfcalc wins the damage-formula audit, narrowly

**wfcalc has the most correct code of the four calculators.** Of the 28 disputed points it gets **23 right**. The other five are a deliberate omission (Madurai), a precision detail worth at most 0.3% (base crit multiplier quantization), and three real status-effect errors: Slash bleed skipping shields, an uncapped Gas stack count, and Electricity/Gas ticks starting one second late. Void-forge comes second with **18 right**. Its errors are spread over headshots, armour floors, Overguard, DoT seeds and Puncture. warframe-damage.com is clearly last with **8 right**. It still runs several pre-Update-36 or pre-Update-40 rules: 1/16 quantization, flat Heat strip, 6 s Puncture, an old Techrot curve and a wrong faction table. Underframe is correct on all 12 points that can be read from its JavaScript and wrong only on a harmless stat-rounding detail. Its hit, armour, status and DoT maths live in a compiled WASM module that was not run, so **15 of its 28 points are unknown** and it cannot be ranked overall. Every verdict below rests on wiki.warframe.com, read as raw wikitext on 2026-10-29, and on DE patch notes quoted there. The wiki is community-maintained, and several of the rules that decide verdicts were written by a single editor in July 2026 with no DE statement behind them. Those rules are marked medium confidence.

## How the 28 points were judged

Each point is checked against the current wiki text and, where one exists, the DE patch note behind it. "Right" means the code matches what the wiki says the game does today. "Partial" means it matches for some factions or some statuses. "Omitted" counts as wrong when the mechanic changes damage. Underframe (UF) is "?" wherever the number sits in `dps_calculator.wasm`. Its input fields show which mechanics it models (`affectedByArmor`, `statusCaps`, `heatInheritFactionPercent`), but not the numbers it uses. Abbreviations: VF = void-forge.org, WD = warframe-damage.com.

## Weapon side: quantization and headshots separate the sites

The weapon-side split turns on whether a calculator tracked **Update 40**. Hits are quantized: each physical and elemental type rounds to "the nearest multiple of 1/32nd of their attack's base damage, before being multiplied further". Faction and final multipliers "only multiply final quantized values" ([Damage/Calculation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Calculation?action=raw)). The change from 1/16 to 1/32 appears in a 2025-11-12 wiki revision whose edit summary credits U40 ([revision history](https://wiki.warframe.com/api.php?action=query&prop=revisions&titles=Damage/Calculation&rvprop=ids|timestamp|user|comment&rvlimit=200&format=json)). No DE patch note mentions it, so the date is medium confidence, but later detailed edits keep /32. WD's step formula multiplies the step size by faction and final multipliers. That looks wrong but is harmless, because scaling the value and the step by the same factor cancels out. **Its only real error is the divisor.** Under /16, small residual physical portions round to zero that /32 would keep.

The base-crit quantization in point 2 is real but tiny. The wiki stores base CD as a 12-bit code with steps of 32/4095, applied before mods ([Critical Hit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Hit?action=raw)). Computed from that formula, the error is +0.12% at CD 1.6, −0.15% at 1.8 and +0.17% at 2.2. It never changes the ranking of two builds on the same weapon. UF and VF model it; wfcalc does not. That costs wfcalc a point on the scorecard but almost nothing in output.

Headshots are where **Void-forge and WD diverge most from the game**. The wiki puts the base headshot multiplier at 3× for most factions, but "for The Murmur and Techrot, the usual multiplier is closer to 1.5x". Its Techrot table ranges from a 1× Scaart head to a 4.5× Skuzzi monitor ([Enemy Body Parts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Body_Parts?action=raw)). wfcalc's 1.5× for both is the best single faction default. VF's 3× for Techrot is right only for Obsolyte and Legacyte heads. WD's 3× for the Murmur is plainly wrong.

The doubled crit headshot is "applied more narrowly and literally than the headshot flag". It covers Grineer, including their Corrupted, Narmer and Anarch variants, plus Dax Anarchs, Scaldra and most Infested and Sentients. It excludes "all Corpus humanoids and ground units, most of the The Murmur, and much of Techrot" ([Enemy Body Parts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Body_Parts?action=raw)). wfcalc's three-faction exclusion matches this at faction level. VF wrongly denies headcrits to Corrupted Grineer, Narmer units, Archons and Zariman Thrax, which all have 3× heads that do headcrit. WD gives headcrits to Corpus, which overstates Corpus headshot damage by about ×1.8 at CD 4.

No calculator models Madurai correctly. Phoenix Talons adds 0.3 × the weapon's *total* base damage, elemental included, as flat physical damage after physical mods. It is not a ×1.3 multiplier ([Focus/Madurai](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Focus/Madurai?action=raw)). WD's double ×1.3 (≈×1.69) is the worst of the four, because it overstates the bonus in every case. Leaving it out, as wfcalc and VF do, understates damage only for players who run Madurai.

## Enemy side: WD runs outdated tables, VF trips on edge cases

On the enemy side **wfcalc, Underframe and Void-forge mostly agree**, and the wiki mostly backs them. WD diverges on seven of the twelve points. Its faction table gives Sentients a Magnetic weakness and Scaldra a Toxin resistance. The wiki's overview table, its damage-type data module and both faction pages say Sentients are weak to Cold and Radiation and resist Corrosive, and Scaldra resist Gas ([Damage/Overview Table](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Overview_Table?action=raw), [Damage/Scaldra](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Scaldra?action=raw)). WD's Techrot health curve (`0.015q^2.1 → 13.64q^0.72`) differs from the wiki's `0.02q^2.12 → 15.0998q^0.7`. Hotfix 38.0.8 "increased the overall damage and Health of the Techrot faction" ([Techrot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Techrot?action=raw)), so WD's figures are plausibly pre-hotfix values, though that is an inference. WD's Sentient shield curve `2q^0.75` is the wiki's Corrupted/Anarchs curve, not the Sentient one, which is `1.6q^0.75` ([Enemy Level Scaling](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Level_Scaling?action=raw)). For Eximus, WD multiplies a neutral curve onto every faction and also scales armour. The wiki instead raises the base and then applies the unit's own faction curve, and it publishes no Eximus armour formula at all.

The armour points split the field differently. The wiki gives DR = `0.9·√(armor/2700)` up to 2700. Above 2700 it uses `armor/(armor+300)`, which applies only when buffs push armour past the level-scaling clamp; the two formulas meet at exactly 90% ([Armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor?action=raw)). wfcalc and VF implement both branches and WD clamps. The >2700 branch, though, is a single July 2026 edit with no patch note behind it, so this verdict is medium confidence. The same page states that "each damage type has a minimum damage of 1" against armour, with a Braton example. **VF is the only site without that floor.** It costs VF very little except against very high armour, where low-damage types would otherwise round to near zero.

VF's other two enemy-side slips are small. An enemy whose faction is spelled "Orokin" rather than "Corrupted" falls to the default curves. Its Overguard smoothstep uses the level rather than `level − 1`, which the wiki explicitly says "ignores the enemy's individual base level" ([Enemy Level Scaling](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Level_Scaling?action=raw)). That makes it one level off. All four sites agree on Steel Path: since U36 it multiplies health and shields by 2.5 and no longer touches armour ([The Steel Path](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Steel_Path?action=raw)). Stat rounding is a non-issue, because the wiki stores scaled stats as binary32 and integer rounding moves them by less than one point.

## Status and DoT: wfcalc is current on formulas, behind on shields and Gas

wfcalc's DoT formula is the only one that matches the wiki's current Damage/Calculation page. That page defines `Unrounded Tick Damage = (Σ S_i + 1) × C × M`, where the accumulator "starts at 1 rather than 0". It gives worked examples such as "(40 × 1.55 + 1) × 0.5 × 3.25 × 1.55 = 158.68, displayed as 159" ([Damage/Calculation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Calculation?action=raw)). VF and WD follow the older formulas, which are still printed without the +1 on the per-element pages such as [Slash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage?action=raw). The +1 comes from one editor's July 2026 edits, which an admin later left intact, and no DE source confirms it. It shifts ticks by about 2.5% at a seed of 40 and by almost nothing at high seeds, so the stakes are small either way. All three readable sites agree that faction damage applies twice to DoT ([Damage over Time](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_over_Time?action=raw)).

**Puncture, Heat and Cold favour wfcalc and VF.** The wiki gives Weakened a 10 s duration in three places, against VF's and WD's 6 s ([Puncture](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Puncture_Damage?action=raw)). Older sources say 6 s and no patch note for the change was found, so confidence here is medium-high. Heat strips armour on a 15/30/40/50% ramp every 0.5 s and decays one step per 1.5 s ([Heat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage?action=raw)). WD's flat 50% overstates the strip during the first two seconds. The Cold bonus is +0.10 at one stack, +0.05 per further stack, and +1.0 when Frozen ([Cold](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Cold_Damage?action=raw)). WD is 0.05 low at every stack count below 10.

**wfcalc loses three status points, and they matter more than its weapon-side misses.** DE's U27.2 notes say "Slash Status Effects will now deal damage over time to Shields", while Toxin keeps bypassing them ([Shield](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield?action=raw)). Only VF models this. wfcalc and WD send bleed straight to health, which overstates Slash against shielded Corpus. Gas caps at 10 stacks, with new stacks replacing the oldest ([Gas](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Gas_Damage?action=raw)). Only WD gets this right, so wfcalc and VF overstate sustained Gas DPS at high proc rates. Electricity and Gas tick at 0–5 s with "no delay", against 1–6 s for Slash, Heat and Toxin ([Damage over Time](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_over_Time?action=raw)). Total damage is unchanged, but wfcalc's kill times for Electricity and Gas builds come out one second late. On Toxin, wfcalc's Treasurer and Hound exceptions match the wiki, and VF has none.

## Conclusion

The calculators split along wiki edit dates more than along engineering quality. A cluster of 2025–2026 edits moved the reference: the U40 1/32 divisor, the 12-bit crit code, the DoT +1 and the >2700 armour branch. WD predates most of them, and VF caught some. wfcalc tracks the current Damage/Calculation page most closely. That also makes it the most exposed if those single-editor edits turn out wrong: the +1 and the >2700 branch each have no DE statement and no linked test. They are the two rules to re-verify in game before treating them as settled. The quickest wins for wfcalc are three status fixes (Slash on shields, the Gas cap and early first ticks). They change real build rankings against Corpus and for Gas builds, which none of the weapon-side precision work does. Underframe stays the open question. Running its WASM against the same 15 test cases would tell whether a fifth reference exists that agrees with wfcalc, or whether it has already found something the wiki has not.
